1.Definitions
For the purposes of this Agreement: “Platform” means the Sheplates digital platform and related services operated by
Sheplates Private Limited. An affiliate may participate in providing services only where appropriately authorised.;
"Vendor” means any individual, sole proprietorship, partnership, limited liability partnership, company, or other
legally recognised person or entity that registers with Sheplates to list, market, offer, sell, or fulfil products or services
through the Platform, subject to applicable eligibility requirements and laws; "Listing Module" means the business
listing model; "Ordering Module" means the order-enabled marketplace model; and "Operations Team" means the
Sheplates team responsible for onboarding, operational support, and escalations.

2.Purpose
This Agreement governs the Vendor's participation on the Sheplates platform, including listing, ordering,
commercial terms, operational responsibilities, and the rights and obligations of both Parties.

5. Vendor Eligibility - FSSAI Requirement
During onboarding, the Vendor shall provide a valid FSSAI registration or licence, as applicable to the Vendor's
food business, or an FSSAI application reference for preliminary verification.
Before activation for food sales on Sheplates, the Vendor must provide a valid FSSAI registration or licence
applicable to its business, unless otherwise permitted by applicable law. An application reference alone shall not
be treated as proof of an approved registration or licence.
The Vendor shall maintain a valid FSSAI registration or licence and ensure timely renewal, where applicable. If
an application is rejected, a registration or licence expires, or the Vendor otherwise fails to meet applicable food
safety requirements, Sheplates may immediately suspend or remove the Vendor's food listings or ordering
facilities without prior notice where reasonably necessary to protect customers or comply with applicable law.
Any withholding of Vendor payouts shall be governed by Clause 11 and Clause 28.5, as applicable.

The Vendor shall ensure that all submitted information is accurate and valid and promptly notify Sheplates of

5.3. Data Security, Retention and Deletion
Sheplates shall implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect vendor documents and

8. Customer Communication
Ordering Module vendors shall not communicate directly with customers under any circumstances. All
order-related communication shall be coordinated exclusively through the Sheplates Operations Team. Listing
Module vendors communicate directly through WhatsApp. Any attempt by Ordering Module vendors to bypass
the Platform and solicit customers directly shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement.

9. Order Cancellations, Refunds and Payouts
Customer cancellation or modification requests shall be handled in accordance with Sheplates' applicable
cancellation and refund policy. Before Vendor acceptance, such requests must be routed through the Sheplates
Operations Team. Vendor cancellations after acceptance must also be routed through the Sheplates Operations
Team.

If a Vendor cancels an accepted order, Sheplates shall notify the customer and initiate a refund of the amount
paid for the cancelled order to the customer's original mode of payment, in accordance with the applicable
refund policy. The cancelled order shall be excluded from the Vendor's payout, and no settlement shall be made
to the Vendor for that order. If the amount has already been included in a payout calculation or settled, Sheplates
may adjust or recover the corresponding amount from subsequent payouts, subject to applicable law and this
Agreement.
10. Operational Performance and Remedies
The Vendor shall maintain accurate inventory, update order statuses promptly, prepare orders within committed
timelines, fulfil accepted orders within the selected delivery or collection slot, and comply with service
standards communicated by Sheplates.

10.1 Stock-Related Cancellations: If a Vendor cancels more than three (3) orders in a calendar week of Monday
to Sunday due to stock unavailability, the Vendor's account shall be automatically deactivated. The count shall
apply separately to each Vendor account. The Vendor must contact the Sheplates Operations Team to request
reactivation, which shall be subject to verification and approval by Sheplates.

10.2 Service Failures and Financial Penalties: Failure to meet applicable service-level agreements (SLAs) or
operational standards may result in corrective action, financial penalties or suspension, in accordance with the
applicable policies communicated by Sheplates. Any financial penalty shall be imposed only where the triggering
event, penalty amount or calculation method, and applicable conditions have been communicated to the Vendor
in advance. The Vendor shall be notified of the penalty and may raise a dispute through the Sheplates Operations
Team.

10.3 Poor-Quality Orders: For verified orders involving poor product quality, Sheplates may require the Vendor
to reimburse the value of the affected products, subject to verification, notification to the Vendor and applicable
law. The Vendor may dispute the decision through the Sheplates Operations Team.

10.4 Repeated Operational Failures: Repeated failures to comply with operational standards may result in
proportionate corrective action, suspension or deactivation in accordance with this Agreement and applicable
policies.

11. Payments & Settlements
Customer payments are collected online. Vendor settlements are processed every Wednesday for successfully
completed orders from the previous Monday to Sunday.

Vendor settlements shall be subject to deductions for agreed commissions, applicable subscription fees,
customer refunds, and other amounts due under this Agreement that are reasonably documented.

Sheplates may temporarily withhold the amounts reasonably necessary to address pending customer disputes,
chargebacks, suspected fraud, regulatory requirements, food-safety or compliance concerns under Clause 5, or
other legitimate risks, subject to applicable law. Any undisputed balance shall be settled in accordance with the
applicable settlement schedule, subject to applicable law.

12. Subscription Fees
Subscription plans may be billed monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly, as offered by Sheplates. The applicable
subscription plan, billing period, subscription fee and taxes shall be communicated to the Vendor when the
Vendor selects or renews a subscription. Monthly subscription rates and included features are set out in
Annexure A.

Where subscription fees are agreed to be deducted from Vendor payouts, any shortfall must be paid by the
Vendor within 24 hours of notification. If the outstanding amount remains unpaid, Sheplates may suspend the
relevant subscription features or the Vendor's access as permitted under this Agreement.

16. Data Privacy
The Vendor shall use customer information solely for fulfilling orders placed through the Platform and shall

17. Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Agentic Commerce
Sheplates may use Artificial Intelligence (AI), automated systems and Agentic Commerce technologies to
facilitate product discovery, customer recommendations, marketplace operations and the generation of business
insights for Vendors.

When made available by Sheplates for certain plans, Vendors may access insights relating to their listings,
products, customer preferences, demand patterns, sales performance and other relevant business information.

Such insights are provided solely to help Vendors understand and improve their business performance. Vendors
shall not attempt to access the underlying AI systems, customer data, confidential platform information or
insights relating to other Vendors unless expressly authorised by Sheplates.

Vendors shall use the insights only for legitimate business purposes connected with their participation on
Sheplates and shall not copy, disclose, sell or misuse any personal information or confidential information
contained in them. Vendors must comply with this Agreement, Sheplates' applicable policies and applicable data
protection and privacy laws.

Sheplates may modify, restrict or discontinue AI-powered insights or related features from time to time. Insights
are provided for informational purposes and may not always be complete or accurate. Vendors remain
responsible for their own business decisions and for complying with their obligations under this Agreement.

19. Indemnity

28. Agreement Term and Termination

28.1 Term: This Agreement shall remain in force for a minimum period of three (3) months from the Effective
Date, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Clause.

28.2 Termination by the Vendor: After the minimum term, the Vendor may terminate this Agreement by
providing at least one (1) month's prior written notice to Sheplates.
28.3 Termination by Sheplates for Convenience: Sheplates may terminate this Agreement for convenience at
any time, including during the minimum term specified in Clause 28.1, by providing at least fifteen (15) days’
prior written notice to the Vendor.

28.4 Immediate Suspension or Termination: Sheplates may suspend the Vendor's listings, ordering facilities
or account, or terminate this Agreement immediately, where reasonably necessary due to food safety or
regulatory non-compliance, fraud, material breach of this Agreement, repeated operational failures,
non-payment of amounts due, or other circumstances presenting a material risk to customers, Sheplates or the
Platform. Where appropriate, Sheplates may notify the Vendor and provide a reasonable opportunity to remedy
the breach before termination.

28.5 Outstanding Payments and Final Settlement: Upon termination, all outstanding amounts payable by the
Vendor shall remain due. Sheplates may temporarily withhold only the amounts reasonably necessary to address
pending customer refunds, chargebacks, payment disputes or other documented liabilities, subject to applicable
law. Any undisputed balance shall be settled within forty-five (45) days of termination, or earlier where required
by law.

28.6 Continuing Obligations: Termination shall not affect rights, obligations or liabilities accrued before the
termination date. Provisions that are expressly stated to survive termination, or which by their nature require
continued effect, shall remain in force.
